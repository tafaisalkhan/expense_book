Privacy Policy for Expense Book

Effective Date: September 29, 2026 | Application: Expense Book (com.myexpense.book)

Offline-First Promise: Expense Book is designed with privacy at its core. All your expenses, budgets, profiles, and receipts are stored locally on your device by default.

1. Overview

Expense Book ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This policy details how our app handles data, permissions, and advertising for com.myexpense.book.

2. Data Collection & Processing

A. Local Financial Data

Expense records, categories, monthly budgets, and profile labels remain strictly inside a local SQLite database on your device and are never uploaded without your manual export action.

B. Google Authentication

Google Account email and display name are used solely for identity verification during login via Firebase Auth.

C. SMS Parsing Permissions (READ_SMS, RECEIVE_SMS)

Used strictly on-device to auto-detect whitelisted bank transaction notifications with user confirmation. SMS text is never transmitted off your device.

D. Location Permissions (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION)

Used strictly on-device for location-based expense prompts when visiting commercial areas (petrol stations, supermarkets). Coordinates are never tracked or stored externally.

3. Advertising & In-App Purchase

The app uses Google AdMob (com.google.android.gms.ads) for ads. Users can purchase the Remove Ads product (remove_ads - $4.99) to disable all advertising permanently.
