Privacy Policy for Expense Book

Effective Date: November 24, 2026 | Application: Expense Book (com.myexpense.book)

Offline-First & Private Cloud Backup Promise: Expense Book stores your expenses, budgets, profiles, and receipts locally on your device by default, with optional encrypted Cloud ZIP backup for signed-in Cloud Backup subscribers.

1. Overview

Expense Book ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This policy details how our app handles data, cloud backups, on-device OCR/Voice features, permissions, and advertising for com.myexpense.book.

2. Data Collection & Processing

A. Local Financial Data & On-Device OCR / Voice

Expense records, categories, monthly budgets, receipt photos, and profile labels are stored in a local SQLite database on your device. Receipt text recognition (OCR) and 10-second voice-to-expense recognition run on your device.

B. Optional Cloud ZIP Backup & Restore

When signed in with Google with an active Cloud Backup subscription (cloud_backup_monthly_v2 up to 100 MB, or cloud_backup_yearly up to 600 MB), your expenses and receipt attachments can be backed up manually or on an automatic schedule as a compressed ZIP archive (latest.zip) to our private cloud storage scoped strictly to your authenticated account UID over HTTPS/TLS.

C. Google Authentication

Google Account email, display name, and UID are used solely for identity verification, subscription entitlement checks, and isolating your private Cloud ZIP backup.

D. Device Permissions

RECORD_AUDIO is used only when you tap Voice Expense for 10-second speech recognition. Camera/Photo access is used only when scanning or attaching receipts. POST_NOTIFICATIONS, VIBRATE, and USE_BIOMETRIC support reminders and optional app lock security.

3. Advertising, In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

The app uses Google AdMob (com.google.android.gms.ads) for free-tier ads. Users can purchase Remove Ads (remove_ads - $4.99) or subscribe to Cloud Backup ($0.99/month or $5.99/year with a 7-day free trial) via Google Play Billing to disable all advertising and unlock cloud backup.

4. Data Deletion & Right to Erasure

You can permanently delete your account, local records, and your Cloud ZIP backup archive at any time inside the app via More → Delete Account (which purges your cloud ZIP file and metadata immediately), or by contacting us at the email below.
