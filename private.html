Privacy Policy

Application: MyExpense (com.myexpense.app)  |  Effective Date: September 25, 2026

At MyExpense, we prioritize user data privacy, security, and complete transparency. This Privacy Policy outlines how our application collects, processes, and protects your information when you use our mobile expense tracking application, OCR document scanner, SMS financial transaction categorizer, and map-based geofence location notifications.

1. Information Collection & Usage

A. Camera & Storage Access (OCR Receipt Scanner)

  • Purpose Enables users to take photos of paper receipt slips, store bills, or attach PDF receipts for automated extraction of line items, amounts, merchant name, date, and time.
  • Data Protection Optical character recognition is processed locally on your device. Captured images are stored securely in local device storage and are never sold or used for targeted advertising.

B. SMS Reading Permission (RECEIVE_SMS)

  • Purpose Analyzes incoming financial transaction SMS notifications from verified banks and payment systems to automatically prepare draft expense entries for user review.
  • Data Protection SMS parsing takes place entirely on your device. Non-financial SMS messages are discarded immediately. SMS contents are never transmitted to remote servers or third parties.

C. Location Services & Geofencing (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION)

  • Purpose Displays interactive OpenStreetMap location pickers, supports custom geofence pin creation (e.g. Petrol Stations, Supermarkets, Markets), and triggers local reminders upon departure.
  • Data Protection Geofence coordinates remain stored locally in your device app state. Location data is never broadcasted to tracking networks.

D. Local Data Storage & Control

  • All expense records, budget limits, category assignments, and family member tags are stored in encrypted SQLite storage on your physical device.
  • You retain complete ownership of your data with options to edit, export to CSV/PDF, or purge all records at any time.

2. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do NOT sell, trade, rent, or commercialize your personal or financial data under any circumstances. External map tiles are rendered using OpenStreetMap public tile servers without transmitting user identity or financial details.

3. Security Standards

MyExpense incorporates strict platform security features, including local database isolation, permission-sandboxed runtime Android rules, and HTTPS transport layer security for optional network requests.

4. User Rights & Data Deletion

You can manage permissions (Camera, SMS, Location) anytime through Android OS settings. You may permanently delete your data by clearing app data or uninstalling the app.
