Privacy Policy

Application: Expense Book (com.myexpense.book)  |  Effective Date: October 9, 2026

At Expense Book, we prioritize user data privacy, security, and complete transparency. This Privacy Policy outlines how our application collects, processes, and protects your information when you use our mobile expense tracking application and OCR document scanner.

1. Information Collection & Usage

A. Local Financial Data (Offline-First)

  • Purpose Expense amounts, categories, monthly budgets, and profile records are saved locally on your device in a secure SQLite database.
  • Data Protection Data remains strictly on your device and is never uploaded without explicit user backup/export action.

B. Camera & Storage Access (OCR Receipt Scanner)

  • Purpose Enables users to take photos of paper receipt slips or attach PDF receipts for automated extraction of line items, amounts, merchant name, date, and time.
  • Data Protection Optical character recognition is processed locally on your device. Captured images are stored securely in local device storage and are never sold or shared.

C. Google Authentication

  • Purpose Google Sign-In email and display name are used solely for account identity verification via Firebase Auth.

2. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do NOT sell, trade, rent, or commercialize your personal or financial data under any circumstances. Non-personal advertising data may be processed by Google AdMob unless disabled via the "Remove Ads" in-app purchase.

3. Security Standards

Expense Book incorporates strict platform security features, including local database isolation and permission-sandboxed runtime Android rules.

4. User Rights & Data Deletion

You can manage permissions (Camera, Storage, Notifications) anytime through Android OS settings. You may permanently delete your data by clearing app data or uninstalling the app.
